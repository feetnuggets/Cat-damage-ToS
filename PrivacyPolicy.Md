#  Cat Damage Bot Privacy Policy

**Cat Damage Bot • “We don’d’a steal’a your’a data’a”**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cat Damage Bot may collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 1. Information We Collect

Cat Damage Bot may collect information necessary for the bot to function, such as:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server IDs
* Channel IDs
* Command usage
* Bot configuration settings
* Basic moderation or error information

Cat Damage Bot does **not** intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, or private messages unless specifically required for a feature.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by Cat Damage Bot may be used to:

* Provide and operate bot commands
* Save server settings
* Prevent abuse and spam
* Diagnose errors and bugs
* Improve the bot
* Maintain security

We do not use collected information to secretly track users.

## 3. Message Content

Cat Damage Bot only processes message content when it is necessary for a specific bot feature or command.

We do not intentionally store private conversations or messages indefinitely.

## 4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to:

* Operate Cat Damage Bot
* Protect the bot from abuse
* Comply with applicable laws or legal requests
* Protect the rights and security of the bot and its users

## 5. Data Storage

Information may be stored for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Cat Damage Bot's features.

Server owners may request deletion of configuration data associated with their server.

## 6. Data Deletion

If you want data associated with your Discord account or server deleted, contact the owner of Cat Damage Bot.

Please include enough information to identify the relevant server or account.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Cat Damage Bot operates through Discord and may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Cat Damage Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users should follow Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.

If the policy changes, the updated version will be made available through the Cat Damage Bot's official information channels.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns regarding Cat Damage Bot, contact the bot's owner through the official support/contact method.
